Overview

Gunung Jerai offers a network of trails ranging from casual walks to challenging hikes. Trekking through this highland ecosystem allows you to witness unique flora, including exotic orchids and pitcher plants, set against a backdrop of ancient geological formations.

Some trails lead to hidden waterfalls like Alur Naga, while others traverse historical sites. It is highly recommended to hire a local guide for deeper trails to ensure safety and gain insights into the rich biodiversity.

What to Bring

  • Proper hiking shoes with good grip.
  • Light, breathable clothing (and a light jacket for the cool peak).
  • Sufficient drinking water and high-energy snacks.
  • Insect repellent and basic first-aid supplies.

Ready to Explore?

For safety, trekking requires a registered guide. Add this to your itinerary or check with local operators.
