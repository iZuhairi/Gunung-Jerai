Getting Here

Gunung Jerai is accessible via the North-South Expressway. Exit at Gurun and follow the winding 13km mountain road to the peak.

GPS Coordinates 5.7950° N, 100.4347° E
🎒

Safety & Preparation

  • Wear appropriate gripped footwear for trails.
  • Check weather conditions; roads can be slippery when wet.
  • Engage registered guides for deep jungle trekking.
  • Bring a light jacket; evening temperatures drop significantly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a permit to climb?

For standard tourist areas and botanical parks, no permit is needed. However, deep jungle trekking requires permission from the Forestry Department.

Are there dining facilities at the peak?

Yes, there are cafes and a resort restaurant available, though bringing your own water and light snacks is recommended.

Contact & Emergency

For official inquiries, contact the local forestry department or your respective resort operators.
